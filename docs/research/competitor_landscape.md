# Competitive Landscape: AI-Assisted Career Platform for Nigerians (job boards, AI CV builders, job matching/alerts, upskilling), 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-10-20. Method note: direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) were blocked by the sandbox network policy (HTTP 403 on CONNECT), so every finding below comes from web-search result summaries of the cited pages rather than first-hand page reads. Prices are therefore "as indexed" and the observation date is the date the search engine's snapshot indicates (given where known). All prices should be re-verified on the live pricing pages before being put in a deck or model. Search budget was exhausted before the final planned queries (Otta/Welcome to the Jungle, Indeed/LinkedIn alert mechanics, Udemy/Alison/FutureLearn, Opportunity Desk, Oyinbo Jobs); those are listed as gaps.

## Key Question 1: Nigerian and African job platforms (features, traffic, pricing, sourcing, weaknesses)

### Takeaway
Jobberman (ROAM Africa / Ringier One Africa Media) remains the highest-ranked Nigerian job site (Similarweb Nigeria rank #212, Sept 2026) and is the only incumbent already selling a naira-priced AI career tool to job seekers (NGN 5,200/week, 12,000/month, 35,000/3 months). MyJobMag (#287) and Hot Nigerian Jobs (#297) are close behind on traffic and compete on cheap/free employer posting (MyJobMag from NGN 7,500/post; free tier) and aggregated listings. The recurring user complaints across all of them are no response from employers, stale/filled listings, and scam postings slipping through.

### Inferences
- Jobberman is the only Nigerian incumbent with a direct B2C subscription analogue (NGN 5,200/week to NGN 35,000/quarter for an AI CV/interview tool). A new entrant's naira price point must be benchmarked against NGN 12,000/month and against MyJobMag's one-off human CV rewrite at NGN 30,000-80,000.
- The incumbents' revenue is overwhelmingly employer-side (NGN 7,500-100,000+ per post; NGN 450,000/yr unlimited). Job-seeker monetisation is small and recent, suggesting low willingness-to-pay is assumed by incumbents, or an untested opportunity.
- With ~2,400 applicants per listing (ROAM figure), "no response" is structural; a product that filters and ranks fit before the user applies addresses the root complaint better than more listings.
- TalentQL/Andela/Gebeya are B2B talent outsourcers, not direct competitors for a B2C CV/alerts/learning subscription; they matter only as potential distribution partners.

## Key Question 2: Nigerian AI/career startups (AI CV, interview prep, job matching)

### Takeaway
A cluster of small, mostly bootstrapped Nigerian AI career tools has emerged in 2024-26 (Abeni AI, HirePadi, Ophy AI, CareerGo, ReviceMyCv, HireScore for employers), typically selling credits or free tiers rather than subscriptions; none found has disclosed funding. The one Nigerian-founded venture-backed player, Sorce (YC F25, swipe-to-apply with AI agent), targets the US market, not Nigeria.

### Cited Findings
- **Abeni AI** (myabeni.com): scores a CV against a job posting (0-100 "job fit score" in under 30 seconds), lists missing keywords and fixes, skills-gap report, AI mock interviews; CVs "stay on the user's device"; credit packs, not subscription: Starter NGN 5,500 for 15 analyses (intl ~$7/19 analyses), Growth NGN 8,500 for 30 (~$14/39), Pro NGN 12,500 for 60 (~$27/78, adds mock interviews and priority support); two free sessions, no card; homepage indexed ~1 year ago; also hosts an "Open Jobs" page — [Abeni AI](https://myabeni.com/); [Abeni AI jobs](https://myabeni.com/jobs); [Abeni AI app](https://www.myabeni.com/app)
- **HirePadi** (hirepadi.com): "AI Job Matching & Application Tracker for Nigerian Professionals"; features listed: AI Match Scores, CV Tailoring, Cover Letter Generator, Application Pipeline, Recruiter Reply Detection, Interview Prep, Daily Job Digest; free tier, no card, structured data lists price 0 NGN; pulls listings from Adzuna, MyJobMag, Jooble, JSearch, Reed, Jobicy, Remotive and LinkedIn (via browser extension), refreshed every 6 hours — [HirePadi](https://www.hirepadi.com/); [HirePadi blog: AI job search in Nigeria](https://www.hirepadi.com/blog/ai-job-search-nigeria); launch listing on [UIComet Launches](https://launches.uicomet.com/products/hirepadi-qV7Ug)
- **Ophy AI** markets an "AI CV Builder for Nigeria — ATS-Optimised Resume for Bank & Tech Jobs" — [Ophy AI NG](https://ophyai.com/ng/resume-builder) (pricing not captured)
- **CareerGo** markets an "AI Resume Builder Nigeria" — [CareerGo NG](https://careergo.co/ng) (pricing not captured)
- **Resumly** has a Nigeria landing page for an AI resume builder — [Resumly Nigeria](https://www.resumly.ai/ai-resume-builder-nigeria)
- **ReviceMyCv**: free CV analysis, NGN 2,000 rewrite/tailor — [ReviceMyCv](https://revicemycv.com/cv-tailor)
- **HireScore** (hirescore.com.ng): employer-side AI CV scoring to "hire 10x faster" — [HireScore](https://hirescore.com.ng/)
- **Profolio.ng**: Nigerian CV samples/guides (e.g., AI Engineer CV sample 2026) — [Profolio](https://www.profolio.ng/ai-engineer/cv-sample)
- **CareerLead AI** (Ghana/Africa): AI career guidance; says it partners with Jobberman, BrighterMonday and MyJobMag — [CareerLead AI](https://careerlead.ai/)
- **Sorce** (formerly Swype): founded Aug 2024 by Lagos-raised Oluwapelumi Dada (CEO), Daniel Ajayi (CTO), David Alade; swipe-based job app where an AI agent fills applications and writes cover letters; YC F25 batch; first funding from Hugo Thieblot and Founders Inc.; angels include Jobberman co-founder Opeyemi Awoyemi and AltSchool founder Adewale Yusuf; round size undisclosed; reported ~500,000 users and 20M+ swipes (unverified; sources vary); went viral in the US — [Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/feature/ai-swipe-for-jobs-viral/); [Innovation Village](https://innovation-village.com/sorce-joins-y-combinator-the-tinder-for-jobs-built-by-three-nigerians/); [Techmoonshot, Dec 2025](https://techmoonshot.com/2025/12/15/three-young-nigerian-immigrants-went-from-college-students-to-yc-backed-founders-with-sorce/); [Accretio Africa](https://accretio.africa/nigerian-founders-redefining-global-job-search/); [Sorce LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sorcejobs)
- Adjacent Nigerian HR/talent raises 2025: Raenest (cross-border pay for African freelancers) raised an $11M Series A extension — [Techgix: nine Nigerian startups funded 2025](https://techgix.vercel.app/post/9-nigerian-startups-that-gained-funding-in-2025); Semicolon (talent/training) ~$1.3M total funding — [EdTech Startups: upskilling](https://www.edtechstartups.org/top/upskilling/)
- Aibase.ng lists "10 exciting African AI products 2025" (no career tool confirmed from snippet) — [Aibase.ng](https://aibase.ng/ai-trends/african-ai-products-2025/)

### Inferences
- The Nigerian AI-career niche is crowded at the "free CV scorer" layer but no player has visibly combined (a) AI CV improvement, (b) emailed curated job matches, (c) a learning path, and (d) a subscription. HirePadi is closest (match scores + daily digest + free tier) but is free and aggregator-sourced; Abeni sells credits only.
- Credit-pack pricing in the NGN 5,500-12,500 range (Abeni) and Jobberman's NGN 12,000/month suggest a market-tested ceiling of roughly NGN 10,000-15,000/month for consumer career tools.
- Venture capital for Nigerian B2C career tools appears thin: the only funded Nigerian-founded team (Sorce) chose the US market, implying investors doubt Nigerian consumer willingness-to-pay.

### Gaps
- No funding, traction or founder data found for Abeni AI, HirePadi, Ophy AI, CareerGo, ReviceMyCv or HireScore; no TechCabal/Techpoint/Disrupt Africa coverage of any of them surfaced.
- No evidence found on failed Nigerian AI career startups (what happened to those that shut down) — searches returned nothing specific.
- Sorce's round size and valuation undisclosed.

## Key Question 3: Global AI CV/job tools and pricing (free tiers, email digests, monthly USD)

### Takeaway
Global AI CV builders cluster at $19-40/month with heavy discounting on quarterly/annual plans (Kickresume ~$24/mo or $96/yr; Enhancv $39/mo or $99/6 mo; Rezi $29/mo or $149 lifetime; Teal+ $29/mo; Jobscan $49.95/mo). Resume.io and Zety monetise via $1.95-2.95 trials that auto-renew at $25.95-29.95 every four weeks, which is the single biggest source of complaints. Job trackers/auto-appliers (Huntr $40/mo, Careerflow $23.99-44.99/mo, Simplify+ ~$39.99/mo, LazyApply $99-999/yr, Sonara ~$24/mo) exist, but none in the results sells a curated emailed job digest as the core paid product.

### Inferences (gaps a new entrant could own)
1. Combined loop (CV -> matched jobs -> learning path when CV is weak -> re-match): no player found does all three; HirePadi (free, aggregated) and Jobberman (paid CV tool, separate job board) are each one step away.
2. Trust layer: verified employers, listing freshness/expiry, "no fee before interview" policy, and scam flagging directly answer the top complaints; incumbents acknowledge cloned/scam listings slip through.
3. Truly personalised digest: existing Telegram/WhatsApp channels (up to ~54K subscribers) and board alerts are unpersonalised; a fit-scored, few-per-week digest via email plus WhatsApp/Telegram is unoccupied.
4. Naira pricing and billing transparency: global tools are $20-50/month in USD with trial traps and card/FX friction; Jobberman anchors NGN 12,000/month; a NGN 5,000-15,000/month plan with local payment rails (cards, transfer, USSD) and cancel-anytime is a clear positioning.
5. CV tuned for Nigerian employers/ATS: only MyJobMag (human) and Ophy/Abeni (AI) market "Nigerian ATS" tuning, and no public evidence exists on how Nigerian employers actually screen; a platform with employer-side data (via partnerships or its own postings) could own this credibly.
6. Learning path to free/cheap content (ALX, 3MTT, Google certificates) keeps the subscription affordable while differentiating from catalogue-driven course sites; affiliate revenue from AltSchool/Utiva/Coursera is a secondary monetisation route.
7. Distribution partnership rather than competition with TalentQL/Andela/3MTT placement portal (supply of pre-screened candidates).

### Gaps
- No market-sizing or willingness-to-pay survey data for Nigerian job seekers paying for career tools was found.
- No evidence on how Nigerian employers use ATS (which systems, penetration), so the "tuned for Nigerian ATS" claim rests on vendor marketing only.
- Live pricing pages were not read directly; every price should be re-verified before publication.
- Not researched due to search budget: Otta/Welcome to the Jungle, Udemy/Alison/FutureLearn, Opportunity Desk, Oyinbo Jobs, Careers24, BrighterMonday specifics, Hiration, iOS App Store reviews, Reddit threads, X threads, Africa: The Big Deal and Crunchbase funding lists for Nigerian career tech.
