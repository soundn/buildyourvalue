# ADR-0019: Define tiers by service level, never by kind of job; Pro replaces the Remote tier

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-26
- Deciders: Founders
- Related: BRD §14.1, BR-015, BR-021, BR-033, BR-061, BRL-010, BRL-019; PLAN.md §Price at ₦1,999; ADR-0017, ADR-0018

## Context
ADR-0017 replaced Pro with a "Remote" tier and ADR-0018 priced it at ₦9,999. Both left the top tier named and described by the kind of job, which blurred two separate things: the Nigeria-eligibility filter, a safety feature that every user needs the way they need the scam screen, and the amount of service a subscriber buys. The founder's position is that the board is one price list for every kind of job, and that a higher subscription buys more service, not a different shelf of jobs.

## Decision
Tiers are defined by service level only. Every tier, Free included, shows every eligible job, local and remote, and no feature is gated on a job's location (BRL-019). The top tier is Pro at ₦9,999 a month or ₦24,999 a quarter: everything in Core plus unlimited tailoring with a fair-use cap of 20 a month, tailoring to the user's career path, mock interviews, the WhatsApp digest, priority re-match, and the global-CV and getting-paid modules whenever the user's targets are abroad. Those modules belong to the learning path and switch on from the user's targets, not from a tier. Free, the ₦1,999 pass and Core at ₦1,999 are unchanged. The revenue model's "remote share" becomes the Pro share; the figures stand.

## Alternatives considered
| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Keep a Remote tier | Prices by kind of job, paywalls a safety feature, and tells a nurse in Kano the top tier is not for her |
| A single paid tier | Loses the ₦1,999 entry point the domestic market can afford and the ₦9,999 the committed seeker will pay |
| Price Pro lower now that it is not "remote" | Pro bundles tailoring, interviews and a coached path; Jobberman charges ₦12,000 for a CV tool alone. ₦7,500 stays as the Phase 0 test arm |

## Consequences
- Positive: one message for the whole market; the eligibility filter reads as protection rather than a product; the people chasing dollar roles self-select into Pro for its services, so the willingness to pay ADR-0018 relied on is kept.
- Negative or risks: ₦9,999 must be justified by service, which the pre-order page tests; documents that defined the tier by job type had to change again.
- What would make us revisit this: Pro converting below two thirds of the ₦7,500 arm in Phase 0, or usage data showing Pro buyers are almost entirely remote seekers, which would argue for a remote-specific add-on inside Pro rather than a separate tier.

## Verification
Nothing numeric changed; the revenue model runs unchanged under the new label. The tier definitions are in BRD §14.1 and the pricing page.
