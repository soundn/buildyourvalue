# Ladda brand guide (v0.1, 24 October 2026)

Product brand: **Ladda**. Tagline: **Build your value**. Parent line: **a SoundTechWorks
solution**. "Build Your Value" stays the working name in code and documents until the
domain, CAC, trademark and social-handle checks below pass; the brand files here are ready
to switch over the moment they do.

Shareable brand reference (tokens, logo files, voice, app-store identity): the Ladda design
system artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/5JMKVKc6hSg3CKf5NuBUFV (private until shared
from its Share menu). This folder stays the source of truth; regenerate the artifact when the
files here change.

## Why Ladda

Ladda is the phonetic spelling of "ladder": the product's whole promise is a next rung
(CV score, matched jobs, learning path, re-match), and the mark is literally a ladder with
the top rung highlighted. The name is two syllables, pronounceable in every Nigerian
language, carries no fintech or church connotation, and travels to Ghana and Kenya. The
research shortlist and reasoning are in `docs/PLAN.md` (Naming) and
`docs/research/gtm_and_naming.md`. Runner-up: Afara (Yoruba for bridge). Nigeria-only
option: CareerPadi.

What a web search found on 24 October 2026: no company, app or startup named Ladda in
any career or software category. The word is Swedish for "to charge" and appears in
Swedish EV-charging programmes and a Gothenburg utility app ("Ladda Din Elbil"). Those
are a different class of goods and a different jurisdiction; they are not a conflict for
Nigerian classes 35, 41 and 42, but they do mean `ladda.com` and `ladda.se` are unlikely
to be free. Nothing was checked against a registry because the research environment could
not reach one.

anything until the CAC and trademark searches are clean; a domain is cheap to lose, a
rebrand after launch is not.

## Logo

Files in `brand/logo/` (SVG, editable) and `brand/exports/` (PNG, generated by
`brand/render-brand.mjs` with Playwright's Chromium: `node brand/render-brand.mjs`):

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `ladda-mark.svg`, `ladda-mark-{1024,512,192,48}.png` | App icon, favicon, avatar. Green tile, white ladder, gold top rung. |
| `ladda-mark-light.svg` | Mark on white or pale backgrounds (green ladder, gold rung), no tile. |
| `ladda-lockup.svg`, `ladda-lockup-1440.png` | Primary horizontal lockup: mark + wordmark + tagline, for light backgrounds. |
| `ladda-lockup-dark.svg`, `ladda-lockup-dark-1440.png` | Same lockup on ink background, for dark UI, social headers, slides. |
| `ladda-wordmark.svg` | Wordmark only. |

Construction: the mark is a 256-unit tile with 56-unit corner radius. The ladder leans 17
degrees to the right (climbing), two rails at stroke 16, three rungs at stroke 14, all with
round caps. The top rung is gold; the others white. Minimum size: 24 px for the mark, 120 px
wide for the lockup. Clear space: half the tile width on all sides.

The wordmark is set in Manrope ExtraBold at -4 tracking (Inter ExtraBold is the fallback
and is what the PNGs here were rendered with, since Manrope was not installed). Before the
wordmark goes on anything printed or into the app stores, open `ladda-lockup.svg` in Figma,
set Manrope, and outline the text to paths so the file no longer depends on a font.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#0F6B3A` | Primary. Buttons, mark tile, links. Chosen over fintech blues and the national green-white-green because it reads as growth without looking like a bank or a party. |
| Forest dark | `#0B4F2B` | Hover and pressed states. |
| Sun | `#F2B418` | Accent. The top rung, step numbers, highlights. Never for body text on white (contrast fails). |
| Ink | `#14211B` | Text, dark backgrounds. |
| Muted | `#5B6B63` | Secondary text. |
| Surface | `#F4F7F5` | Page background. |
| Line | `#DDE5E0` | Borders. |

Contrast: Forest on white 7.4:1, Ink on Surface 15:1, white on Forest 7.4:1, Ink on Sun
9.6:1. Sun on white is 1.9:1 and is for shapes only.

These are the same tokens already in `frontend/src/app/globals.css`, so the web app is
on-brand without a change.

## Typography

Headings and wordmark: Manrope (open licence, Google Fonts). Body and UI: system font stack
(`ui-sans-serif, system-ui`) to keep the PWA light on low-end Android; Manrope loads only
for the landing page headline. Numbers in tables use tabular figures.

## Voice

Direct, concrete, warm. Short sentences. British English spelling. No exclamation marks, no
"unlock", no "journey", no emoji in product copy. Say what the number is and what to do
about it ("Your CV scores 44. Add a number to each bullet."). Pidgin is welcome in marketing
when it is natural, never forced; product copy stays in plain English. The three lines that
appear on every surface:

- We never charge you to apply, and no employer on Ladda may charge you.
- Every listing is screened for scams before you see it.
- A SoundTechWorks solution.

## App store identity

- Name: Ladda. Subtitle: Jobs you can actually get.
- Android package / iOS bundle id: `ng.soundtechworks.ladda` (the Expo scaffold uses
  `ng.soundtechworks.byv` until the name is confirmed; change once, before the first
  store upload, because it cannot change afterwards).
- Icon: `ladda-mark-1024.png`. Android adaptive icon: foreground = white ladder with gold
  rung on transparent, background = Forest.
